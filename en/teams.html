Our philosophy

We don't organize events. We design experiences.

Human connection doesn't happen by accident. It can be intentionally designed. Every experience we create is crafted to help people communicate better, build trust, and form relationships that matter.

Most team events focus on the activity. We focus on what happens between people. When someone feels safe, present and genuinely there, something shifts: walls come down, real conversations happen, trust begins to form.

Before we start

Frequently asked questions

What team building activities do you organize in Barcelona?

We organize human connection experiences, creative workshops and customized team building activities. We combine group activities, guided conversations and shared experiences to strengthen trust, communication and collaboration. Every activity is tailored to your company's goals.

How long does a team building activity last?

Our experiences can last from one hour to several hours, depending on what your team needs. We can run a standalone session or build our activities into a company day. We design the program to make the most of the time available.

Can you organize team building for remote or hybrid teams?

Yes, our in-person experiences are especially valuable for bringing together colleagues who usually work remotely. We create spaces where they can get to know each other beyond video calls and day-to-day tasks. We can adapt the activities for teams meeting for the first time or teams that already work together.

Can the team building take place at our office?

Yes, we can bring the experience straight to your office or to a venue of your choice. We can also help you find a suitable location in Barcelona. We take care of facilitating the activities, so your team only has to take part.

How much does team building with Happy to Gather cost?

The price depends on the number of participants, the duration and the type of experience. Since every team has different needs, we prepare customized proposals. Tell us what you're looking for and we'll help you find the right format.
